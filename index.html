Hitodor Chat

Private messaging where trust belongs to people, not the platform.

Your contacts are people you choose. Someone joins your conversations only by accepting your invitation, or by a request from your card that you approve.

  • Messages, photos, video and voice notes are end-to-end encrypted.
  • Circles for group conversations.
  • No phone number or email needed to start. Your identity lives on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Forwarding needs the sender's approval, and received messages can't be copied or shared out of the app.
